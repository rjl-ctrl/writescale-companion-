Informed Consent — Research Participation Notice

This assessment is part of a research study examining doctoral writing development. Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without consequence.

All data collected through this assessment will be fully confidentialized. Your name will not appear in any research findings, publications, or presentations. If you choose to participate, your written response and assessment scores may be used for scholarly research purposes under these confidentiality protections.

This study is conducted by Dr. Rodney Luster, University Research Chair, Center for Leadership and Entrepreneurial Research (CLER), University of Phoenix.
